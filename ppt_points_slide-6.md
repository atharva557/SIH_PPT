## RESEARCH & REFERENCES

### Research Foundation

- **RemoteCLIP (2023)** — Vision-language foundation model specifically developed for remote sensing; supports image–text retrieval and zero-shot applications.
- **ChangeFormer (2022)** — Transformer-based Siamese architecture for change detection from co-registered multi-temporal remote-sensing imagery.
- **OSCD – Onera Satellite Change Detection (2018)** — Sentinel-2-based benchmark with pixel-level change annotations for evaluating urban change detection.
- **Cross-Domain Change Detection Review (2024)** — Establishes registration, feature representation, similarity-based detection and environmental/sensor differences as major challenges in change detection.
- **Deep Learning Change Detection Survey (2025)** — Reviews current datasets, architectures, evaluation methods and remaining challenges in remote-sensing change detection.
- **CLIP (2021)** — Foundational vision-language approach demonstrating image–text alignment and zero-shot visual retrieval/classification.

### Data & Quality Foundations

- **ESA/Copernicus Sentinel-2** — Multispectral Earth-observation data with global coverage and approximately 5-day revisit.
- **Sentinel-2 Cloud Probability / Cloud Score+** — Established quality layers for identifying clouds, shadows and usable pixels.

### Research Direction

**Our approach combines established research components — semantic vision-language retrieval, multi-temporal change detection, image registration, quality masking and evidence fusion — into a unified offline analyst workflow.**
