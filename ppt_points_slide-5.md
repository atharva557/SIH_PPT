For the **Impact & Benefits** slide, I’d keep it outcome-focused and formal, with four clear areas:

## IMPACT & BENEFITS

### Faster Intelligence

- Rapid retrieval of relevant satellite imagery using **natural-language or image queries**.
- Reduces time spent manually searching large imagery archives.

### More Reliable Change Detection

- Identifies meaningful changes across multiple dates while reducing **false alarms** from clouds, shadows, seasonality and registration errors.
- Provides confidence scores and supporting evidence for each detected change.

### Better Analyst Decision Support

- Combines **before/after imagery, change masks, temporal evidence and provenance** in a single workflow.
- Enables analysts to verify findings rather than relying on opaque automated results.

### Secure & Scalable Deployment

- **Offline and on-premises** operation supports sensitive or connectivity-constrained environments.
- Incremental ingestion and modular processing enable the system to scale with growing satellite archives.

### Overall Impact

**Transforms large satellite archives into searchable, verifiable and evidence-driven intelligence — reducing manual effort while improving the speed and transparency of change analysis.**
